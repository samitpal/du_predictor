About this tool. Data is drawn directly from the University of Delhi's official UG Seat Matrix and CSAS minimum allocation score bulletins. For 2026‑27: the Seat Matrix (3 Jul 2026), Round 1 (17 Jul 2026), Round 2 (25 Jul 2026) and Spot Round (27 Aug 2026). For 2025‑26: Round 1 (19 Jul 2025), Round 3 (13 Aug 2025) and Spot Round (28 Aug 2025) — note 2025‑26 has no Round 2 file and no published Seat Matrix in this tool, so seat counts aren't shown for that year, and its category list is narrower (UR, OBC‑NCL, SC, ST, EWS, PwBD only). It is an independent, unofficial reading aid built to help applicants get a feel for where they stand — it is not affiliated with the University of Delhi, and allocation depends on many factors (seat availability, preference order, tie‑breaking rules, and any rounds not covered here) beyond a single score. Always confirm against the official Admission Branch bulletins at admission.uod.ac.in before making decisions.
